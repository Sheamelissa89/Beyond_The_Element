Quiz

Is water an element or a compound?

Reveal answer

A compound. Water is H₂O, containing hydrogen and oxygen.

Human
Elements

Our bodies contain many chemical elements. These six account for roughly 99% of body mass:

  • Oxygen (O) — in water and many biological molecules.
  • Carbon (C) — forms the framework of organic molecules.
  • Hydrogen (H) — in water and organic molecules.
  • Nitrogen (N) — in proteins and DNA.
  • Calcium (Ca) — supports bones, teeth, and cell signaling.
  • Phosphorus (P) — in bones, DNA, and energy-carrying ATP.

Smaller amounts of elements such as iron, sodium, potassium, and magnesium also play essential roles.

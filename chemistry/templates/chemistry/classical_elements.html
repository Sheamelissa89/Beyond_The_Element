Question Classical elements Chemical elements
What are they? Categories within historical explanations of nature. Types of atoms identified by their proton count.
Examples Earth, water, air, and fire; aether in Aristotle’s celestial account. Hydrogen, oxygen, carbon, iron, and others in the periodic table.
How is water described? A fundamental substance in this classical framework. A compound: H₂O.

What identifies an element? Its role within the historical theory. Its atomic number: the number of protons.

Check Your Understanding

Think through each question before opening its answer.

Is mercury a metal, a liquid, or both?

Both. Mercury is classified as a metal and is liquid at approximately 20°C under ordinary atmospheric pressure.

Is water a chemical element?

No. Water is a compound with the formula H₂O, containing hydrogen and oxygen.

What does an element's atomic number tell us?

It tells us the number of protons in the nucleus of each atom of that element.

Does melting iron turn it into a different element?

No. Melting changes its physical state. It remains iron because its atomic number stays the same.

Why are classical air and modern oxygen different ideas?

Classical air was treated as a fundamental substance. Modern chemistry identifies air as a mixture and oxygen as one of the chemical elements in that mixture.
